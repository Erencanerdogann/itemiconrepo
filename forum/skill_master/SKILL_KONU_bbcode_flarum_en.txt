[CENTER][SIZE=22][B][COLOR=#c8a253]⚔️ 05 · MASTER QUEST (2ND JOB) — ALL JOBS[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=18][B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Level 60+[/COLOR][/B] · everyone collects [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]1 Unstable Kentaraus' Heart[/COLOR][/B] + [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]2 Essences[/COLOR][/B] specific to their job and brings them to the master NPC → you become [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Master[/COLOR][/B].[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18][B][COLOR=#e6c46a]How to do it — step by step:[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=18][COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]1 ·[/COLOR][/B] Go to your job's NPC (section 04) → talk → take the [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]master quest[/COLOR][/B] — the quest name is *2nd job change* (Kurian: *Second* · Porutu: *2nd job*)
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]2 ·[/COLOR][/B] Kill [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Centaurs[/COLOR][/B] → [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Unstable Kentaraus' Heart[/COLOR][/B] drops (50%) — purple dots on the map
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]3 ·[/COLOR][/B] Kill your job's [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]2 field bosses[/COLOR][/B] → [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]2 Essences[/COLOR][/B] drop (100%) — which boss: section 06
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]4 ·[/COLOR][/B] Go back to the NPC with the 3 items → hand in the quest → you're [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Master[/COLOR][/B], and the [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Master[/COLOR][/B] tab opens in the [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]K[/COLOR][/B] window[/SIZE]

[CENTER][SIZE=22][B][COLOR=#c8a253]📜 07 · 70 – 80 SKILL QUESTS[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=18]After you become Master, [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]the same master NPC[/COLOR][/B] teaches the hidden skills. Each quest asks for [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Spell Stone Powder[/COLOR][/B], and the reward is [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]that level's skill[/COLOR][/B].[/SIZE]

[CENTER][IMG]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Erencanerdogann/itemiconrepo@main/forum/skill_master/resim_en/S05_skill_gorev.1a8fcf1c.jpg[/IMG][/CENTER]

[SIZE=18][B][COLOR=#e6c46a]How to do it — step by step:[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=18][COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]1 ·[/COLOR][/B] First become [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Master[/COLOR][/B] (section 05)
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]2 ·[/COLOR][/B] Collect as much [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Spell Stone Powder[/COLOR][/B] as your job's total — where from: section 08
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]3 ·[/COLOR][/B] Go to the same NPC → take the [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]70Lv skill[/COLOR][/B] quest → hand in the powder → that skill unlocks
[COLOR=#c8a253]◆[/COLOR] [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]4 ·[/COLOR][/B] Then the others for your job: 72Lv · 74Lv · 75Lv … [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]80Lv skill[/COLOR][/B] — at Level 83 you can take them all on the same day[/SIZE]
