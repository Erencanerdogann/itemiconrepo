## ⌨️ 03 · SKILL WINDOW (K)

**K** → Skill window. Every job has **4 tabs**, the last one is **Master**. You assign points with the **▲** next to the tab counter.

- **Warrior** — Attack · Defence · Passion · Master
- **Rogue** — Archery · Assassinate · Search · Master
- **Mage** — Fire · Ice · Lightning · Master
- **Priest** — Heal · Aura · Spirit · Master
- **Kurian** and **Porutu** — Attack · Defence · Devil · Master

![Rogue example: Assassinate 80 + Search 48 + Master 20 = 148 points]({{R07}})
▲ *Rogue example: Assassinate 80 + Search 48 + Master 20 = 148 points*

💡 To put points into the Master tab, first finish the **Master quest**.

## ⚔️ 05 · MASTER QUEST (2ND JOB) — ALL JOBS

**Level 60+** · everyone collects **1 Unstable Kentaraus' Heart** + **2 Essences** specific to their job and brings them to the master NPC → you become **Master**.

![]({{S01}})

**How to do it — step by step:**

- **1 ·** Go to your job's NPC (section 04) → talk → take the **master quest** — the quest name is *2nd job change* (Kurian: *Second* · Porutu: *2nd job*)
- **2 ·** Kill **Centaurs** → **Unstable Kentaraus' Heart** drops (50%) — purple dots on the map
- **3 ·** Kill your job's **2 field bosses** → **2 Essences** drop (100%) — which boss: section 06
- **4 ·** Go back to the NPC with the 3 items → hand in the quest → you're **Master**, and the **Master** tab opens in the **K** window

📘 Open in guide: [Warrior](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/408) · [Rogue](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/409) · [Mage](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/410) · [Priest](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/411) · [Kurian](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/784) · [Porutu](https://sexyko.com/guide/quests/781)

## 📜 07 · 70 – 80 SKILL QUESTS

After you become Master, **the same master NPC** teaches the hidden skills. Each quest asks for **Spell Stone Powder**, and the reward is **that level's skill**.

![]({{S05}})

**How to do it — step by step:**

- **1 ·** First become **Master** (section 05)
- **2 ·** Collect as much **Spell Stone Powder** as your job's total — where from: section 08
- **3 ·** Go to the same NPC → take the **70Lv skill** quest → hand in the powder → that skill unlocks
- **4 ·** Then the others for your job: 72Lv · 74Lv · 75Lv … **80Lv skill** — at Level 83 you can take them all on the same day

## 💠 08 · WHERE TO GET SPELL STONE POWDER?

![]({{S06}})

![]({{S07}})

**♻️ Spell Stone Powder with Narki** — turn accessories you don't use into Spell Stone:

![]({{S09}})

- **Where:** Moradon · **[Mysterious] Narki** (4 of them, all the same) — 849,660 · 780,660 · 680,538 · 825,620
- **How:** talk to Narki → give your accessory (**one at a time**) → the accessory **disappears** → **1 reward** is drawn in return
- **Old accessories** pool (Old … accessories, 64 kinds) → 1 – 5 Spell Stones, average **≈ 2.4**
- **Accessories** pool (normal accessories, 926 kinds) → 1 – 5 Spell Stones, average **≈ 1.6** · 23.17% chance of a Forgotten Accessory Box
- **Not allowed:** bound, rented, sealed or timed items can't be given · an exchange can't be undone · you need free space in your inventory

**[📘 Open in guide: Narki Exchange (search which pool your accessory falls into) ↗](https://sexyko.com/guide/narki)**
