🧞 04 · ADVANCED GENIE

◆ 8 Attack + 8 Buff/Self + 8 Party skill slots — each row can be turned on / off separately
◆ Set HP and MP potions by percentage (Smart Auto Use)
◆ Party heal and self heal thresholds · Attack range slider
◆ Monster List to Attack — Add Target / Remove / Clear All: Genie only hits the monsters on the list

[IMAGE R03: Knight Genie — Main tab]

◆ Combat: attack with R · go to the target · return to the hunting center
◆ Maintenance: repair with a hammer · keep the premium flash bonus · feed the pet
◆ Return to town: when the party breaks up · when the party dies · when arrows run out

[IMAGE R04: Knight Genie — Misc tab]

💡 While Genie is on, the skill bar is locked; stop it to go back to manual play.

🏆 19 · EVENT RULES

◆ Knight Chaos: 18 players, free, 20 minutes — everyone plays the same character with 10,000 HP; most kills and fewest deaths get ahead
◆ Border Defense War: 8v8, 30 minutes — carry the altar to your base for 80 points, a kill is 2 points; first to 600 points wins
◆ Juraid Mountain: 8v8 PvPvE, 50 minutes, Lv 70+ — the nation that kills Devabird wins
◆ Forgotten Temple: 80 players, 100,000 Noah — [Priest] Iris (Moradon 815,921), monsters coming in waves
◆ Castle Siege War: clan war for the Delos castle, 60 minutes — sign-up 100,000,000 Noah
◆ Knight Royale: 80-player battle royale, everyone starts at level 1
◆ Under the Castle: Lv 75+, 4 bosses — your equipment burns when you die, carry a Life Crystal
