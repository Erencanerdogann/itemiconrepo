[CENTER][SIZE=22][B][COLOR=#c8a253]🎙️ 10 · SPONSOR STREAMER APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[CENTER][IMG]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Erencanerdogann/itemiconrepo@main/forum/yayinci/resim_en/V05_sartlar.44ebec11.jpg[/IMG][/CENTER]

[SIZE=18]🎙️ [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]SexyKO Sponsor Streamer Program[/COLOR][/B] — The SexyKO Sponsor Streamer Program was created to build long-term partnerships with streamers who stream regularly, with quality and actively. Applications are evaluated by channel quality, audience, stream history and activity. Limited spots are available.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]📊 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Followers and Views[/COLOR][/B] — The applicant must have at least 250 followers on YouTube or Kick. A regular stream history and a real audience are also expected. Besides follower count, average viewers, stream engagement and channel activity are also evaluated.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]⚔️ [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]SexyKO Content[/COLOR][/B] — The main focus of the streams must be SexyKO. Sponsor streamers are expected to stream SexyKO regularly and show the server's gameplay and systems to their viewers. In streams: PK, farming, events, competition, character progression, item upgrading and SexyKO-exclusive systems.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]🛡️ [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Clean Stream History[/COLOR][/B] — The applicant's stream history must not include platform bans, cheating, scams, serious community violations or abuse of server rules. SexyKO management has the right to review past streams and channel history when it sees fit.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]📢 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]SexyKO Promotion on Stream[/COLOR][/B] — Sponsor streamers are expected to include SexyKO in the stream title and actively promote the server during the stream. Using SexyKO links in the stream description/panels and keeping the required sponsor visuals on the stream is mandatory.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]🎤 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Microphone Use — MANDATORY[/COLOR][/B] — All streamers accepted into the sponsor streamer program must use an active microphone while streaming. Channels that stream without a microphone are not accepted into the sponsor streamer program.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]💰 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Camera / No-Camera Streamer[/COLOR][/B] — Sponsor streamer payments can vary by stream format and performance. Different payment packages apply to streamers with and without a camera. Camera use, stream quality, activity and performance criteria are considered in the payment evaluation.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]🎁 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Sponsor Streamer VIP Support Pack[/COLOR][/B] — Our sponsor streamers get a 7-day VIP Support Pack as a gift when they start streaming, renewed every week the sponsorship continues. Our partner streamers get a 30-day VIP Support Pack. The packs are more limited than Origin VIP and are given only for in-game support.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]⏱️ [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Weekly Stream Time[/COLOR][/B] — Sponsor streamers must stream at least 20 hours per week. Stream times are checked regularly, and the sponsorship and VIP renewal of streamers below the set time are reviewed.[/SIZE]

[SIZE=18]💬 [B][COLOR=#e6c46a]Active Streaming and Engagement[/COLOR][/B] — Streamers are expected to be active throughout the stream, talk with their viewers and give info about SexyKO. AFK, silent or no-engagement streams are not counted under the sponsorship.[/SIZE]
